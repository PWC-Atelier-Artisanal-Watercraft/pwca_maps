# North America water pack with a zoom-8 level (2026-10-03)

Panoptes (display). Branch `panoptes/na-z8-level`. Why: the owner, 2026-10-02 23:01, "I had said many times, I want the
entire US map." The card's pack (`out\na`, zoom 10 to 16) drew nothing at the display's zoomed-out views (its view
reaches zoom 9), so he saw only the coarse Natural Earth overview from flash.

## What changed in the builder

- `build_pack.py` LEVELS: a fourth level, L0, cut at zoom 8 and serving zooms 8 and 9, 12 coordinate bits, buffer
  32, 150 m simplification; inland areas under 25 ha are left out there (a few pixels at most). L1 to L3 unchanged.
- `fit_tile`: a per-tile budget of 12,000 features or parts (the display's scratch holds 16,384 and skips a bigger
  tile whole). A tile over it loses its shortest lines first, and the build names the tile. In the NA build exactly
  two L0 tiles of the Yukon-Kuskokwim delta were over: z8 13,73 lost 6,534 and z8 12,73 lost 6,136 river pieces of 3
  points or fewer (about 18,000 each before).
- `tools/tile_limits.py`: per level, the largest blob, most points and most features or parts against the display's
  limits (1 MiB, 131,072, 16,384).

## For the card

`python tools\map_pack\install_pack_to_card.py out\na-z8 <card root> --name NA-20260924-Z8` (pwca_argos). The tool
copies the pack, checks every file against MANIFEST.sha256 and switches CURRENT; the old pack stays on the card as the
way back. The display needs no firmware change for this pack: it draws a card water file at any level that serves the
zoom.
