## What changed against the file of 2026-10-03

- The header states version 1.1 (it was 1.0).
- The buffer of the three 12-bit levels is 64 units (it was 32): 4 px where the tile zoom is the display zoom. The
  display draws each tile inside its own square, so a road just outside the square has to be in the tile for half its
  stroke width; the widest stroke at those zooms is 5 px on the 5" glass (2.5 px each side), and 32 units were 2 px.
  About 0.8 MB more data and 1,020 more tiles (tiles that hold only a road in their buffer).
- `full_class` is 0 (roads have no "full" tile).
- The file's name is `north-america-roads.pmt`, so it can sit beside the water file `north-america.pmt` in one pack
  directory on a card.
